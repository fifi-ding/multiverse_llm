Multiverse butterfly

Each universe is one combination of a narrative (how the question is framed), a granularity (how it must be answered), a profile (or none) and a model, run several times. Outcomes are the model's stance on getting vaccinated: answers to negated questions are flipped, and on the 1–5 scale 1–2 = NO, 3 = OTHER, 4–5 = YES.
